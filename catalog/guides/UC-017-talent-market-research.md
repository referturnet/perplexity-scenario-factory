# UC-017: Talent Market & Salary Research

- **Domain:** research / hr · **Difficulty:** basic · **Time:** 20–45 min

## Goal
Benchmark roles, salaries, and talent supply for hiring planning.

## Inputs
Role family; target geography; seniority levels.

## Steps
1. Compensation benchmarks from surveys, listings, levels-style data.
2. Demand signals: posting volume trend, time-to-fill, top hiring companies.
3. Supply signals: graduate output, migration, community sizes.
4. Package norms: base / bonus / equity mix by company stage.

## Verification
Salary figures must cite source and date; ranges conflicting by >30% flagged rather than averaged.

## Expected Output
Benchmark table by level + demand/supply notes + package recommendations.

## Variations
Remote-first benchmark · equity-heavy startup packages.
