# UC-012: Patent & IP Landscape

- **Domain:** research / ip · **Difficulty:** intermediate · **Time:** 45–90 min

## Goal
Map the patent and IP landscape around a technology area or competitor before building or investing.

## Inputs
Technology description; competitor names; jurisdictions of interest.

## Steps
1. Define search axes: technology keywords, assignees, IPC/CPC classes.
2. Surface key patents and applications per competitor; note filing dates and families.
3. Identify white space — sub-areas with little patent activity.
4. Flag litigation history and licensing deals (FRAND, cross-licensing).
5. Synthesis — landscape map: dense zones, white space, risk areas, filing velocity trends.

## Verification
Patent numbers must be cited with a source; confirm at least 3 key patents exist via a second query.

## Expected Output
Landscape map + top-10 patents table + white-space notes.

## Variations
Freedom-to-operate pre-scan · acquisition IP audit.
