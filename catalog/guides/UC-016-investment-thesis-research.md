# UC-016: Investment Thesis Research

- **Domain:** research / finance · **Difficulty:** advanced · **Time:** 60–90 min

## Goal
Build a defensible bull/bear thesis for a company, sector, or asset class.

## Inputs
Subject (company / sector / asset); horizon; risk tolerance.

## Steps
1. Structural drivers — what must be true for the thesis to work.
2. Bull case: strongest evidence with sources and dates.
3. Bear case: disconfirming evidence — actively searched, not optional.
4. Valuation ranges from public methods and analyst estimates, with assumptions.
5. Falsification: list signals that would kill the thesis and when to check them.

## Verification
Every bull and bear claim dated and sourced; at least one source less than 90 days old per side.

## Expected Output
Thesis memo: drivers / bull / bear / valuation / kill signals.

## Variations
Sector rotation scan · pre-IC memo.
