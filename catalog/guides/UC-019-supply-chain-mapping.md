# UC-019: Supply Chain Mapping

- **Domain:** research / operations · **Difficulty:** advanced · **Time:** 60–120 min

## Goal
Map the supply chain of a product or company: tiers, dependencies, risks.

## Inputs
Product/company; region of interest; risk focus (geopolitical, single-source, ESG).

## Steps
1. Tier-1 identification: direct suppliers and manufacturing sites from filings, news, job posts.
2. Tier-2+ inference: sub-suppliers from shipping data, certifications, disclosures.
3. Chokepoints: single-source components, geographic concentration, logistics hubs.
4. Risk overlay: geopolitical exposure, ESG findings, recent disruptions.
5. Synthesis — tiered map + chokepoint list + mitigation options.

## Verification
Tier-1 claims need at least one public source; inferred tiers must be marked INFERRED.

## Expected Output
Supply-chain map + chokepoint table + mitigation notes.

## Variations
Component-level dependency scan · supplier ESG audit.
