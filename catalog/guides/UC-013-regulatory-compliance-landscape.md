# UC-013: Regulatory & Compliance Landscape

- **Domain:** research / regulatory · **Difficulty:** advanced · **Time:** 60–120 min

## Goal
Understand the regulation affecting a product or business in target markets before launch.

## Inputs
Product description; target countries; business model.

## Steps
1. Identify applicable regimes (data protection, AI acts, fintech, medical, e-commerce).
2. Per regime: scope triggers, key obligations, deadlines, enforcement history.
3. Recent developments: pending bills, consultations, regulator guidance.
4. Peer practice: how comparable companies comply.
5. Synthesis — compliance matrix: requirement × jurisdiction × risk × first action.

## Verification
Each obligation must link to the official source or regulator page; secondary summaries are marked as such.

## Expected Output
Compliance matrix + top-5 risks + action checklist.

## Variations
AI-product compliance (EU AI Act, model cards) · data-transfer analysis.
