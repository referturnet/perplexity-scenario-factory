# UC-011: Company Due Diligence

- **Domain:** research / due-diligence · **Difficulty:** intermediate · **Time:** 30–60 min

## Goal
Produce a structured due-diligence brief on a target company before a deal, partnership, or hiring conversation.

## Inputs
Company name + website; deal context (investment / partnership / vendor / hiring); optional red-flag checklist.

## Steps
1. Baseline scan — profile: history, ownership, funding, leadership, products, market position, with sources.
2. Financial signals — revenue estimates, funding rounds, headcount trajectory, layoffs, expansion.
3. Legal & reputation — litigation, sanctions, regulatory actions, press coverage last 24 months.
4. Dependencies — key customers, suppliers, tech-stack signals from job postings.
5. Synthesis — one-page brief: strengths / risks / open questions / recommended next checks.

## Verification
Every factual claim carries a source link; unsourced claims marked UNVERIFIED. Cross-check two key facts with a second query.

## Expected Output
One-page brief + source list + red-flag summary.

## Variations
Vendor diligence (security, SLA, dependency risks) · pre-interview research (leadership, strategy).
