# UC-015: Market Entry Analysis

- **Domain:** research / strategy · **Difficulty:** advanced · **Time:** 60–120 min

## Goal
Evaluate entering a specific country/region with an existing product.

## Inputs
Product; home market; candidate countries.

## Steps
1. Market size and growth with assumptions stated.
2. Competitive density: local and international players, pricing norms.
3. Barriers: regulation, logistics, payments, language, channel structure.
4. Entry modes: direct, partner, distributor, local entity — with cost/complexity comparison.
5. Synthesis — ranked shortlist with entry mode and first-90-days plan.

## Verification
Market-size claims must show the calculation chain; conflicting estimates reconciled or both reported.

## Expected Output
Country comparison table + recommended entry mode + risks.

## Variations
E-commerce market entry · B2B SaaS localization.
