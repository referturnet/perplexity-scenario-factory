# UC-020: Scientific Evidence Scan

- **Domain:** research / science · **Difficulty:** advanced · **Time:** 60–90 min

## Goal
Assemble and grade the scientific evidence around a claim, intervention, or technology.

## Inputs
Question or claim; field of study; recency window.

## Steps
1. Literature sweep: systematic reviews first, then primary studies.
2. Per study: design, sample, effect size, conflicts of interest.
3. Grade the evidence: strength, consistency, direction of findings.
4. Contradictions: map studies that disagree and why (population, method, funding).
5. Synthesis — evidence summary with confidence levels and remaining unknowns.

## Verification
Each study cited by title and link; preprints clearly labeled; sample sizes reported.

## Expected Output
Evidence table graded by strength + confidence statement + open questions.

## Variations
Clinical claim check · nutrition claim check · emerging-tech maturity scan.
